## Long tasks keep going in the background (D-160–D-163, 2026-10-24)

The founder chose option 2: Android does background continuation, and iOS 26 background tasks are tried on the simulator first. The new local module `morph-background`: on Android a foreground service (progress notification, PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK), on iOS `BGContinuedProcessingTask` (off by default). `runner.ts` keeps runs alive before and after runs that contain video or audio steps, the run page hints by platform whether you can switch away, and the video tool page no longer says "Please stay in Morph".

Test on the Android simulator (newly created `morph_background_api35`, Pixel 7 / Android 15, dev build):

- The first run of "Convert to MP4" pops up the system notification permission box; after choosing a video the run page shows "You can switch to another app; Morph keeps working in the background."; after switching to the desktop the notification bar shows "Convert to MP4 · Step 2 of 2: Convert to MP4 · 6%", with a progress bar.
- **Fixed**: this Android 15 image does not accept `mediaProcessing` (`InvalidForegroundServiceTypeException: Starting FGS with type none`), so the service could not start and the progress notification became an ordinary notification that stopping the service could not take away. Now `mediaProcessing` is tried first, with a fallback to `dataSync` on failure (`isForeground=true types=0x1`), and the notification is changed only when the service has really gone to the foreground.
- A 40-second 1080p video: switching to the desktop at 6% of the conversion, locking the screen, `dumpsys deviceidle force-idle` (deep power saving) → finished converting within 30 seconds (24% → 83% → done), and the notification "“Convert to MP4” is done · Tap to see the result."; tapping the notification returns to the result page (0:39, 49.9 MB → 43.2 MB), and the service has already stopped.
- Denying the notification permission (starting again after `pm clear`): it still enters the foreground service and finishes converting in the background, with nothing in the notification bar; "Run again" no longer asks.
- Switching away while choosing a video (the system picker is closed) counts as cancel, and the service stops when returning to Morph; short tasks like images do not start the service.
- On a background failure it sends "“Convert to MP4” didn’t finish". This failure itself was an environment problem: this Mac had a load of 90–137 at that time (several sessions running at once), the simulator CPU froze for 67–84 seconds, system processes were restarted by the watchdog, and the decoding service reported bufferpool errors and `MUXING_TIMEOUT`. For comparison: without the foreground service (the first build), this simulator kept converting for 3 minutes after switching to the desktop as well; the simulator and the Samsung (the 30-second switch-away in D-148) do not freeze in a short time, and the effect of the foreground service only shows with long screen lock, tight memory and vendor power saving.

On the iOS simulator (newly created iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 27, switch turned on temporarily): submitting `BGContinuedProcessingTaskRequest` reports "BGTaskScheduler is not available on this platform", so the **simulator does not support background tasks**; and the simulator does not suspend an App that is switched away (after switching to the home screen for 20 seconds, a 4-minute HEVC still went from 8% to 63% and finished in the end), so the background behavior on iOS can only be judged on a real device. On the simulator it is now treated as unsupported (the run page says "Please stay in Morph"). iOS shows no notification permission.

**Before listing in the store**: in Google Play Console the purpose of the foreground service types `mediaProcessing` and `dataSync` must be declared (with a demo video).

Not tested: speech to text for one hour with the screen locked on the Samsung real device (to see whether One UI power management kills the morph-background service; recording for 1 hour 23 minutes with the screen locked without a break is the recording component's own foreground service, see P-023, and cannot stand in for this item); speech to text in the background (the same keep-alive, covered by automated tests, whisper was too slow on the simulator to run); Android 14 and below (`dataSync`); whether speech recognition can be used in the background on a real iPhone (video compression in the background was confirmed on a real device on 2026-10-31, D-163); after the Android 15 daily 6-hour limit is reached (`onTimeout`).

## Known limitations

- The SQLite storage implementation has no automated tests (Jest cannot run native SQLite), and it was verified on the simulator.
- The hint for encrypted PDFs has no automated test (pdf-lib cannot generate an encrypted PDF as test material).
- In Expo Go, the developer menu button at the top right covers the "✕" of the banner and part of the "Undo" text; the official build does not have this button.
- The banner does not disappear automatically (to keep the "Undo" entry) and has to be closed by hand.
- Android has been verified only on the simulator (T9). On Android the frosted glass card is only translucent, with no blur (D-092).
- Workflow execution uses the device's current time; the dates in generated file names follow the device time zone.
- Files are exported through the share sheet (multiple files at once) or "Save to a folder" (D-119, D-120); there is still no UI for deleting files (deletion needs a separate design and user confirmation).
- Sharing to Morph receives only files, not text and URLs (P-017); an image shared in and saved as a file does not show in "Recent files" (D-051). In the dev build a cold-start share first goes through the dev-client launcher.
