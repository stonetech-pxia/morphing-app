Workshop programme

Monday: Marquetry for beginners, cutting veneers and gluing patterns on boards
Tuesday: Upholstery, stretching webbing and attaching springs to chairs
Wednesday: Woodturning, sharpening gouges and shaping candlesticks safely
Thursday: Gilding, preparing gesso layers and burnishing gold leaf frames
Friday: Restoration, removing old varnish and repairing loose dovetails

Participants bring their own apron and safety glasses; all other tools are provided by the workshop.
