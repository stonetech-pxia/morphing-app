A Regression Page With Two Columns

Left column starts here. The municipal library extended its opening hours during the winter months so that students could prepare for examinations in a quiet and heated room. Volunteers organised reading circles for older residents on Tuesday mornings, and the café on the ground floor offered soup at a reduced price. The board reported that visits increased by a fifth compared with the previous year, and that most new members were under twenty-five years old. This sentence marks the end of the left column.

Right column starts here. The second part of the report describes the renovation of the archive, which had suffered water damage in the spring. Damaged volumes were dried, catalogued and, where possible, digitised so that researchers could consult them online. The archive will reopen in March with new shelving, better lighting and a small exhibition of maps drawn by local surveyors in the nineteenth century. Opening hours and booking rules are published on the noticeboard at the entrance.
