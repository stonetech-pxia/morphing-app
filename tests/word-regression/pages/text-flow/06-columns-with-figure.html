Seasonal Demand at Rural Libraries

Clara Weiss | Working paper 12 | 2026

1 Background

Rural libraries open fewer hours than city branches, yet they often serve as the only public indoor space in a village. This paper looks at how their use changes over the year and which services are borrowed most in each season.

We collected loan records from eleven branches between January and December. Visits were counted at the door, and the staff noted which rooms were used for meetings or homework clubs during the afternoon.
