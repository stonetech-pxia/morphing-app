Notes From the River Survey

Upstream stations

Left column opens here. The upstream stations were visited in early May, when the water was still high after the snowmelt. Samples were taken at three depths and stored in cool boxes until they reached the laboratory in the evening.

Bank erosion

Erosion was most visible on the outer bends, where several willows had fallen into the current. Volunteers planted new cuttings to hold the soil before the summer storms.

Fish counts

Trout numbers were close to last year's, and young grayling were seen for the first time since the weir was lowered. The left column closes here.

Downstream stations

Right column opens here. Downstream, the river was slower and warmer, and the team noted a thin film of algae on the stones near the old mill. Oxygen readings were nevertheless within the usual range.

Water quality

Nitrate levels fell slightly compared with the spring average, which the farmers' cooperative attributes to the new buffer strips along the fields.

Next steps

The survey will be repeated in September with the same stations and methods, so that the two seasons can be compared directly. The right column closes here.
