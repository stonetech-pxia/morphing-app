Information on the programme completed

The programme combines the contents of experimental biology with the methods of the laboratory sciences. Students complete block practicals and internships in research laboratories and in commercial application areas.

Graduates can use their qualification as a prerequisite for a master's programme or start work directly in industry or public research.
