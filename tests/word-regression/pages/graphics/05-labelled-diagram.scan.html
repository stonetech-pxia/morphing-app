2. Water treatment process

The plant draws raw water from the river intake and passes it through four stages before it reaches the distribution network. Each stage is monitored separately, and samples are taken after every step so that a fault can be traced back to the stage where it started.

The diagram below shows the main flow and the return line used for sludge. Operators switch the bypass valve only during maintenance, when one of the filter beds is out of service.

Sludge removed during screening is pressed and dried on site. The filtrate from the press is returned to the intake, which keeps the plant's losses below two percent of the treated volume.

Each filter bed is backwashed once a day. The clear reservoir holds enough water for twelve hours of normal demand, which leaves time to repair a fault without cutting supply.
