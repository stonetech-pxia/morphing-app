Field trip to the Lindau salt marshes

The trip is part of the biology course and is compulsory for both year-nine classes. The details below are also posted on the school website.

Wednesday 18 November: the coach leaves the school gate at 7.45 and returns at about 17.30. Pupils bring a packed lunch, boots and a waterproof jacket.

Journey time: ninety minutes.

The guided walk along the dyke takes two hours. Groups of twelve stay with their teacher and do not leave the marked path at any point of the walk.

18 pupils still owe the consent form.

Your questions can be sent to the class teacher by Friday. A short report on the birds counted during the walk will be shown at the parents' evening in December.

All costs are paid by the school.

Pupils who cannot take part spend the day in the library with an assignment on coastal wetlands.
