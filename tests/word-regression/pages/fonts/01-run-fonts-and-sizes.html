Notice to Residents of Harbour Lane

The water supply on Harbour Lane will be interrupted on Thursday 19 November between nine in the morning and three in the afternoon while the old cast iron main is replaced. Residents are asked to keep a few bottles of drinking water at hand and to avoid running washing machines during the works.

Traffic will be diverted through Mill Street. Parking spaces in front of numbers 12 to 40 will be closed from Wednesday evening, and vehicles left there may be moved by the contractor. Pedestrian access to every house remains open at all times.

Questions about the works can be sent to the neighbourhood office, which is open on weekdays from eight thirty until noon. A second notice will be delivered if the schedule changes because of bad weather.
